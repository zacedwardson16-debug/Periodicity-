Why is argon left out?

These radii are measured from bonded atoms (metallic radius for Na, Mg and Al; covalent radius for Si to Cl). Argon does not form bonds, so its radius has to be measured a different way and is not comparable.

Down a group

Atomic radius increases down a group: Li 152 pm, Na 186 pm, K 227 pm. Each element has an extra shell, so the outer electron is further from the nucleus with more shielding.
